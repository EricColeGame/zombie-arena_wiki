3. **Hoarding Low-Tier Tokens:** Early currencies do not convert directly into high-tier Mutation Cores at favorable rates. Spend baseline tokens immediately to speed up your early clears.
4. **Neglecting Squad Buff Compatibility:** Stacking identical aura perks does not always grant double the bonus. Coordinate with your regular squadmates to ensure your upgrade paths complement one another.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I reset my class skill points if I make a mistake?
Yes. You can respec your allocated perk points at the Barracks terminal. The first respec is typically free, while subsequent resets cost a modest amount of Scrap. However, resetting your perks does not reduce your overall class tier rank.

### Does upgrading a class increase stats for all characters?
Class tier promotions directly enhance only that specific class. However, reaching Tier 3 and completing Prestige milestones unlocks minor account-wide passive perks (such as a 2% baseline stamina increase) that benefit your entire operative roster.

### In Zombie Arena how to upgrade class faster when playing solo?
When playing solo, speed-running lower-tier waves (Waves 1 through 15) on Normal difficulty offers the best return. Running solo matches with precision-focused classes like Marksman or Assault yields rapid XP gains from streak multipliers without having to split elite target kills among teammates.

### What happens to unspent tokens if my class hits the level cap?
Any extra Arena Tokens and Scrap you collect remain in your global inventory. You can use them immediately to upgrade alternate classes, craft advanced weapon attachments, or purchase consumables for upcoming matches.
