## Overview and Core Mechanics of the Celestial Class

The celestial archetype represents the pinnacle of hybrid radiant combat within the arena. Unlike raw physical bruisers or fragile elemental sorcerers, this specialization channels astral power to punish the undead while bolstering party survivability. Every ability scales directly with radiant potency, creating a unique gameplay loop that rewards deliberate positioning and ability chaining.

At its core, the class functions around a dynamic resource system known as Astral Radiance. As you smite waves of infected, your radiance gauge fills, unlocking empowered versions of standard abilities and triggering passive healing pulses. In community reports, players consistently highlight that managing Astral Radiance efficiently separates casual survivalists from leaderboard toppers.

The class also boasts superior passive resistance against corrupted debuffs, toxin clouds, and necro-afflictions that routinely wipe out other character archetypes.

## Advanced Survival Strategies: Mastering Endless Waves

Reaching the upper echelons of wave survival requires more than just high-tier equipment; it demands methodical execution and situational awareness. When piloting the **zombie arena celestial class**, your primary challenge is controlling space and managing your energy economy.

### 1. Master the Perimeter Rotation
Never anchor your character in the center of the arena. Position yourself along the outer lanes, using obstacles to condense swarms into straight columns. This maximizes the piercing damage of your primary radiant projectiles while preventing fast flankers from surrounding you.

### 2. Time Your Cleansing Pulses
Do not waste your cleansing cooldown on minor chip damage. Save defensive auras for moments when armored mutants trigger corrosive puddles or elite shrieker zombies inflict disorientation debuffs. A well-timed cleanse resets the engagement on your terms.

### 3. Ultimate Ability Discipline
It is tempting to unleash your ultimate the instant it finishes charging. However, veteran players reserve *Judgement Descent* for unexpected phase shifts—such as sudden boss spawns, mid-wave enraged berserkers, or emergency corridor extractions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes the zombie arena celestial class unique compared to traditional mages?
While typical arcane spellcasters focus on pure glass-cannon elemental burst, the celestial archetype combines heavy radiant damage with exceptional party sustain, status-cleansing auras, and defensive barriers. It offers significantly higher survivability in chaotic late-game waves where one mistake can end a run.

### Which stats should I prioritize first when leveling up the celestial class?
Early on, prioritize Cooldown Reduction (CDR) and Radiant Damage. As you progress into higher difficulty tiers, weave in Shield Strength and Movement Speed to ensure you can out-kite enraged elites while maintaining high uptime on your defensive barriers.

### Can the celestial class be played effectively in solo survival mode?
Yes. With the Radiant Purifier build, solo players can consistently clear massive hordes. The built-in defensive sustain, crowd-blinding tools, and debuff immunities make this archetype one of the most reliable and self-sufficient classes for solo leaderboard pushes.

### How do I manage Astral Radiance efficiently during endless boss encounters?
Avoid dumping all your high-cost abilities at once. Maintain steady primary radiant attacks to trigger radiance-generating passives, and only spend your reserve on defensive bulwarks or screen-clearing ultimates when the boss enters dangerous enrage phases.
