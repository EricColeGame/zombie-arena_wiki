## Frequently Asked Questions

### Did the zombie arena update change weapon swap and reload cancel timings?
Yes, the zombie arena update modified the animation buffering system across all firearm categories. Weapons now feature distinct reload-cancel thresholds visible on your HUD, enabling players to cut reload recovery animations short by tapping melee or executing a rapid weapon swap once ammo numbers register.

### What is the best sensitivity for controllers in the zombie arena update?
Most players achieve the best balance between precision and responsiveness using an inner stick deadzone between 0.04 and 0.06 paired with a Dynamic response curve. Setting your regular look sensitivity between 6 and 8 while maintaining an ADS multiplier of roughly 0.85x helps you track sprinting zombies without sacrificing 180-degree turn speed.

### Should I use Hold or Toggle for crouching and sliding?
Setting crouch to "Hold" is generally recommended for high-mobility arena shooters. Hold-to-crouch allows you to transition in and out of slide hops instantly, preventing the accidental stance lockouts that often occur with toggle controls during chaotic arena skirmishes.

### How do I fix input lag or dropped keystrokes after the patch?
First, ensure that raw input is enabled in your graphics and input menus, and turn off v-sync or frame buffering options that introduce input latency. If you use a gaming mouse or controller, verify that your device drivers are fully updated and test your polling rate at 1000 Hz for optimal responsiveness.
