### The "Train" Method (Kiting)
Never stand motionless in an arena, even when anchoring a fortified doorway. Move along wide circular perimeters around the arena boundary to bunch the zombie horde into a concentrated column behind you. Once the pack clusters together, turn, fire in sustained bursts into head level, and resume your rotation before stragglers cut off your path.

### Trigger Discipline and Ammunition Economy
Automatic weapons chew through reserves rapidly. Firing in three-to-five-round controlled bursts ensures high bullet accuracy and conserves ammunition crates for higher rounds, when restocking costs become increasingly prohibitive.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes an assault rifle generally considered the zombie arena best gun?
Assault rifles combine balanced handling, deep magazine capacities, swift reload cycles, and manageable recoil profiles. Unlike heavy machine guns that slow your character down or sniper rifles that struggle against swarms, an assault rifle handles both solitary fast runners and dense clusters cleanly.

### Should I prioritize weapon upgrades or defensive perks first?
During the first ten waves, prioritizing weapon upgrades is generally safer because dead zombies cannot deal damage to you. However, by waves 12 to 15, purchasing movement speed and health reinforcement perks becomes essential to survive unexpected hits while maneuvering through narrow corridors.

### Can a shotgun outperform an assault rifle as the zombie arena best gun in late rounds?
Automatic and drum-fed shotguns can surpass assault rifles in raw point-blank burst DPS, making them unmatched inside tight rooms or narrow corridors. However, their sharp damage falloff at medium range makes open-field maneuvering riskier compared to a dependable carbine.

### How do I maintain ammo reserves during endless survival rounds?
Incorporate high-penetration ammunition, aim consistently for headshot hitboxes to minimize bullets per kill, and switch to high-yield secondary weapons during mid-wave pauses. Pairing your primary weapon with ammo-scavenging perks ensures steady crate drops without draining all your banked currency.
