## Advanced Combat Tactics and Wave Survival

### Mastering the Kiting Arc

Never swing while standing completely stationary. Instead, utilize circle-strafing around the edges of the swarm. As the undead cluster together, strike during the inward apex of your turn so that the scythe’s broad 180-degree sweep catches the maximum number of targets.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the primary advantage of using the zombie arena void scythe over blunt weapons?
The **zombie arena void scythe** offers a superior 180-degree horizontal strike arc paired with piercing void resonance waves. While blunt weapons excel at heavy single-target knockdowns, the scythe hits far more targets per swing, generating critical hits and life-leech healing across entire crowds simultaneously.

### Can the void scythe proc special status effects on boss enemies?
Yes. Although major bosses possess high resistance against baseline knockback, community reports confirm that the scythe's void resonance shockwave continues to deal full piercing damage. Furthermore, critical strikes will still trigger your life-steal perks regardless of the target's armor values.

### What is the most effective attack pattern for surviving late-game swarms?
The most reliable sequence is the standard two-light-swing combo followed immediately by a charged horizontal cleave. This sequence triggers the void resonance projectile while keeping your recovery frames short enough to dash away if explosive or toxic enemies detonate nearby.

### Does attack speed gear affect how fast the void shockwave charges?
Yes. Equipping light armor or agility charms that boost attack speed allows you to complete the three-hit combo much faster, thereby triggering the void wave and life-steal procs at an accelerated rate during dense horde waves.
