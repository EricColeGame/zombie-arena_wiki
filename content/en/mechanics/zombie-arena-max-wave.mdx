## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is there an absolute limit to the zombie arena max wave?
In most endless survival modes, there is no coded finale; the engine scales indefinitely until player defeat or an integer overflow occurs. However, reaching an ultra-deep **zombie arena max wave** often becomes functionally impossible as enemy health pools scale into millions of effective hit points, requiring flawless play and continuous kiting.

### What is the biggest mistake players make during late-stage waves?
The most common mistake is camping in stationary bunkers without an exit route. While fortified rooms feel secure early on, high-wave enemy density will overrun any single barricade, trapping players with zero escape options.

### Does cooperative play make reaching the zombie arena max wave easier or harder?
Cooperative play increases aggregate firepower, but it also increases total zombie counts and scaling health pools. Reaching high waves in co-op requires clear communication, designated roles (such as dedicated crowd controllers and boss snipers), and coordinated kite paths so teammates do not steer hordes into one another.

### Should I prioritize armor upgrades or pure movement speed?
Movement speed is universally superior in late-game scenarios. While armor helps absorb incidental chip damage, late-wave mutants strike with such lethal force that armor only buys fractions of a second. Remaining agile enough to avoid taking damage entirely is the key to deep runs.
